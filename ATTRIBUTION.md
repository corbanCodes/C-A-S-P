# Image attribution

Every photograph in `assets/img/` came from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org)
filtered to licences that permit commercial use (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA and public domain), then
cropped, colour-corrected and compressed locally. Each one was reviewed by eye on a contact
sheet before it shipped — the first pass turned up a 13c postage stamp, a WPA war-bonds poster
and a field of horses, none of which made the cut.

Openverse was tried first and now requires an API key, so Commons categories did the work
instead. The two fetch scripts are kept in `_generator/` so the sourcing is reproducible.

## Before launch

These are placeholders that establish the look and prove the layout. A real inspection firm
should be shipping **its own site photography** — Robert on a property with a level and a tape,
actual California storefronts he has inspected, real balcony and walkway conditions. That is
better marketing than any stock library, it is unambiguously licensed, and for this business it
is also evidence of competence. See `DEMO-NOTES.md`.
